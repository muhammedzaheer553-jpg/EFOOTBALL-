Build a realistic 3D football (soccer) game inspired by eFootball using Three.js (WebGL), JavaScript modules and the Web Audio API. Use a multi-file project (index.html, /src, /assets, /data) with clean modules: GameEngine, Renderer, Physics, AI, UI, AudioManager, MatchManager, SaveSystem, TeamDatabase. Target 60 FPS on desktop and mobile with graphics quality settings. === ACTION CONTROLS (eFootball style) === Every action must have separate ball physics, animation and sound. Keyboard / Gamepad (Xbox or PlayStation) / Touch buttons: - Ground pass (short pass): low, fast, accurate. Power scales with button hold. - Lofted pass (up pass): ball goes up and lands near the target teammate. Long range, slower, can be headed. - Through pass: ball is played into space ahead of a running teammate, aimed with the direction stick, receiver auto-runs onto it. - Lob through pass (lofted through ball) when the modifier is held. - Ground shot (low driven shot): low, hard, stays along the grass. - Power shot: hold to charge a power bar, high speed, higher chance of skying the ball when overcharged or under pressure. - Finesse shot: curled shot toward the far post with side spin. - Lob shot (chip) over an advancing goalkeeper. - Headers and volleys when the ball is in the air near the player. - Modifiers: sprint, precision dribble (slow touch), skill moves (step-over, roulette, flick), shield the ball, slide tackle, jockey, switch player, call for a pass. - Shot and pass accuracy depend on player stats, angle, pressure, stamina, weak foot, and how well the ball was controlled. - Show an aim indicator and a power bar. Add camera shake and motion blur on power shots and a short slow-motion on goals. - Include a button-mapping screen and a controls tutorial in training mode. === TEAMS, LEAGUES AND PLAYERS (data-driven) === - Do NOT hard-code teams. Load everything from JSON/CSV files in /data: leagues.json, teams.json, players.json, kits.json, stadiums.json. - Leagues: Premier League, La Liga, Serie A, Bundesliga, Ligue 1, plus national teams (all major countries) with confederation groupings. - Each team: name, short name, league, country, kit colours (home, away, third), stadium, formation, tactics, and a squad of 18+ players. - Each player: name, age, nationality, position(s), preferred foot, weak foot, height, weight, overall rating, and attributes: pace, acceleration, finishing, shot power, long shots, short passing, long passing, vision, dribbling, ball control, defending, tackling, strength, stamina, heading, goalkeeping (diving, handling, reflexes), plus playstyles. - Procedural player models that use each player's height, build, skin tone and hairstyle. Support optional face and badge image slots per player and team. - Squad management: starting XI, bench, formations, set-piece takers, captain, and substitutions. Players tire and get injured. - In-game Edit Mode: edit any team, player, kit, stat and badge. Import and export rosters as JSON/CSV so rosters can be updated when transfers happen. - Ship with a small sample dataset in the same format, so the game works out of the box. Make loading a bigger dataset a matter of replacing the files. - Modes using these teams: Quick Match, League (full season with table and fixtures), Cup, Champions-style tournament, International tournament, and a Master League / Career mode with transfers and finances. === REALISTIC PRESENTATION === - PBR materials, dynamic shadows, bloom, ambient occlusion, weather (day, sunset, night, rain), floodlights and wet pitch effects. - Skeletal-animated players (run, sprint, dribble, pass, shoot, header, volley, tackle, slide, fall, celebrate) with motion blending. - Broadcast cameras (Wide, Tele, Stadium, Pro, Player cam) and goal replays. - Cinematic intro: stadium flyover, tunnel walk-out, line-ups, handshakes, anthem or team music, coin toss, kick-off. - Stadium customisation: pitch pattern, banners, tifo, crowd density, roof, nets, ad boards, with a live-preview editor. - Broadcast overlays: scoreboard, goal banners, cards, substitutions, stats. === AUDIO AND MUSIC === - Dynamic crowd (chants, cheers, groans, boos), kick, net, post, tackle and whistle sounds that change with shot and pass type and power. - Commentary system with context-aware lines (goal, save, near miss, foul) in English, Hindi and Malayalam. - Menu music and in-game playlist, with volume mixer (Master, Music, SFX, Commentary, Crowd). === GAMEPLAY AND AI === - 11v11 with offside, fouls, cards, advantage, penalties, free kicks with wall and curve, corners, throw-ins, goal kicks, half-time, extra time and penalty shoot-outs. - AI with 5 difficulty levels. Teams play to their real-life style (possession, counter-attack, high press) based on the tactics in the data. - Goalkeeper AI that dives, parries, and reads shot type (ground, power, finesse, lob). - Settings: controls remapping, graphics, audio, camera, difficulty, match length, language. Save/load via IndexedDB. === LEGAL NOTE FOR THE CODE === Keep real names, badges and likenesses only in the editable /data files, never hard-coded in the source, so they can be removed or replaced with fictional data before the game is published or sold. Build in phases and test each one: (1) engine, pitch, ball physics, one player; (2) the full pass and shot control system above; (3) database loading and team/player stats; (4) AI and rules; (5) stadium, weather, crowd; (6) cinematic intros, replays, UI; (7) audio, music, commentary; (8) league, cup, and career modes; (9) edit mode and polish.
